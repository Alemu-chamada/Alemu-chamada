### 🌌 About Me

I'm **Alemu Chamada**, a Computer Science & Engineering student at **Adama Science and Technology University** — laser-focused on building intelligent systems at the frontier of software, data, and AI.

My approach is rooted in an **engineering and scientific mindset**: I don't just write code — I architect solutions, model problems mathematically, and deploy systems that are robust, scalable, and purposeful.

**What I'm deeply focused on:**

- 🌐 &nbsp;**MERN Stack Development** — building production-grade, full-stack web applications
- 🤖 &nbsp;**Machine Learning & AI Engineering** — designing models that learn, adapt, and predict
- 📊 &nbsp;**Data Science** — extracting signal from noise with rigorous statistical methods
- 🔬 &nbsp;**Scientific Programming** — simulation, modelling & computational problem-solving
- 🌌 &nbsp;**Astrophysics & Quantum Physics** — applying scientific thinking to engineering challenges
